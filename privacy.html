Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Nepal Unfolded website and publishing tool ("the app") handle. Nepal Unfolded is run by Bijay Bista in Sydney, New South Wales, Australia. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles.

The website

This website has no sign-up, no forms, no ads and no tracking or analytics cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

The app

The app only connects to Nepal Unfolded's own accounts. When we authorise it on TikTok, Instagram or Facebook, it handles:

  • access tokens issued by the platform, so the app can upload to our account;
  • basic account details of our own account, such as account ID, display name and profile picture;
  • details of the videos we upload, such as captions, upload status and post IDs.

The app does not collect information about viewers or other users, and it does not read anyone else's account.

How we use and store it

We use this information only to publish our own videos and to check they were posted. It is stored on our private, access-controlled server and password manager. We don't sell or share it, and we don't use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep tokens only while the app is in use and delete them when we disconnect it. You can remove the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions) or in your Meta Business settings.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Children

The app isn't for use by anyone under 18 and doesn't knowingly collect children's information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
